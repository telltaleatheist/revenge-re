# The package icon

`revenge.png` (512x512), `revenge.ico` (16 to 256) and `revenge.icns` are one
picture: a hawk's head with a sword behind it, inside a crescent, on a riveted
steel patch with gold and bronze inlays. It is this project's own art and
contains nothing from the game, whose DOS release had no icon. It was made the
same way as the icon of Inception Rebuild (this game is
Inception's sequel).

- Made 2026-11-26 with Qwen-Image 2.1 (`qwen-image-2.1`), 768x768, 40 steps,
  seed 72.
- Prompt: "A round military unit patch badge: the head of a hawk in side
  profile facing left, fierce eye, hooked beak, a straight sword crossing
  behind it, inside the curve of a thin crescent moon, embossed brushed steel
  with burnished gold and bronze enamel inlays, inside a thick circular brushed
  steel rim with small rivets around it, symmetrical, front view, centred on a
  pure black background, no text, no letters."
- The black surround was made transparent, the picture cropped square, and
  each size resized; the .icns was made with iconutil.

Where each will be used, when the packages are made: `revenge.icns` for the
Mac app, `revenge.ico` for the Windows program and its installer, `revenge.png`
for the Linux desktop entry and the setup screen's badge.
